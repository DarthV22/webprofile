Fixed Wing UAV Project

Partnership with UoS and Soton UAV

Project details

03

September 2025 – May 2026

Inviscid Suppression of Vortex-Induced Lift

Individual Project with UoS

  • CFD
  • CANARD
  • Python
  • Tecplot 360

Investigated sinusoidal wavy leading-edge geometry for suppressing vortex-induced lift fluctuations through inviscid CFD simulations and automated Python data processing. The study achieved a 42.6% reduction in lift fluctuations.

Project details

  • Conducted high-fidelity inviscid CFD simulations using the CANARD solver via the IridisX HPC cluster.
  • Simulated flat-plate aerofoils at Mach 0.1 and injected a prescribed vortex to model lift fluctuations caused by impact at the leading edge.
  • Investigated the effectiveness of sinusoidal wavy leading-edge geometry and the mechanism of phase cancellation.
  • Developed automated Python scripts to batch-process surface pressure data from Tecplot 360 and compute continuous transient lift histories using numerical methods.
  • Results demonstrated that optimal wavy leading-edge geometries can reliably disrupt vortex coherence.
